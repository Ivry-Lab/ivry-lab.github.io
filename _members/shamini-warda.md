I am a Fulbright Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Psychology at the University of California, Berkeley. My research focuses on human timing and time perception. My current research investigates how distinct attentional subprocesses influence parameters of the internal clock model of timing, as well as the contributions of subcortical structures, particularly the cerebellum and basal ganglia, to temporal processing. My previous research has examined how different predictive processes shape human time perception and the bidirectional relationship between timing and whole-body movement. Outside work, I enjoy practicing and exploring different movement arts and hiking around the Bay Area.
